Password Reset Guide
====================

How to Reset Your Password

If you've forgotten your password, follow these steps:

1. Click "Forgot Password" on the login page
2. Enter the email address associated with your account
3. Check your email for a reset link (it may take 5-10 minutes)
4. Click the link in the email to create a new password
5. Enter your new password (must be at least 8 characters)
6. Click "Save" and you can now log in

Important: Your password must contain:
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one lowercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character (!@#$%^&*)

If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder.
Password reset links expire after 24 hours.
