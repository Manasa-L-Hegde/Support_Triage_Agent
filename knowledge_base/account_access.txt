Account Access and Permissions
==============================

Managing Your Account Access

How to Grant Access to Team Members

1. Go to Account Settings
2. Click "Team Members"
3. Click "Invite Member"
4. Enter their email address
5. Select their role:
   - Admin: Full access
   - Editor: Can create and edit content
   - Viewer: Read-only access
6. Send invitation

Removing Access

1. Go to Account Settings
2. Click "Team Members"
3. Find the member you want to remove
4. Click "Remove" button
5. Their access is revoked immediately

Account Permissions

Different roles have different permissions:

Admin:
- Access to all features
- Invite and remove team members
- View billing and invoices
- Change account settings

Editor:
- Create and edit content
- View team content
- Cannot manage billing or team members

Viewer:
- View content only
- Cannot create or edit
- Cannot manage team or billing

Two-Factor Authentication:
To enable 2FA for better security:
1. Go to Settings > Security
2. Click "Enable 2FA"
3. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app
4. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm
